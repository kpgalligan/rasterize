Three layouts for Rasterize
All three share the same fixes: the pill toolbar becomes a left icon rail with grouped tools under chevrons, square chrome, and a status bar that no longer repeats what the Layers panel already says. They differ in where a tool's options go — the decision that determines whether the canvas ever resizes. Pick one (1a, 1b, 1c) and I'll build it as a clickable hi-fi mockup.
